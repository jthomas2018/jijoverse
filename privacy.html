JijoVerse Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE OF FIRST PUBLICATION]

JijoVerse ("the app"), published by Parables to Promises ("we"), is a daily verse, blessing and prayer app. This policy is simple because the app does not collect anything about you.

What we collect

Nothing. JijoVerse has no accounts, sign-up, analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-reporting or third-party SDKs that gather personal data. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information, device identifiers, location or usage data.

What stays on your device

To make the app work, a small amount of information is saved only on your device, in the app's local storage:

  • verses you save as favorites
  • the days you opened the app (used for your streak)
  • your theme choice, reminder time and whether you have seen the welcome screen

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Narrated prayers

When you press play, the prayer audio file is downloaded from our website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages, and a copy is kept in the app's cache so it can play offline next time. Like any website host, GitHub may briefly process technical information such as your IP address to deliver the file (see GitHub's privacy statement). The narration uses an AI-generated voice. We receive no information about who listens, and the app never uses your microphone.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules a local notification on your device. We do not use push servers, and you can turn reminders off at any time in Settings or in your device settings.

Sharing

When you share a verse card, the app creates the image on your device and opens your device's share sheet. What you share, and with whom, is up to you. We do not see it.

Links

Settings includes a link to our YouTube channel. Pages you visit outside the app are governed by those services' own privacy policies.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Future changes

If a future version adds features that need data (for example online content or optional accounts), we will update this policy and the app store privacy labels before that version is released, and we will explain the change in the app.
